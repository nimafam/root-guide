Wholesale FAQ

Before you place an order.

Can different SKUs be combined to reach MOQ?

Yes. Mixed-SKU orders are part of the current wholesale model.

Do I need to stock all 19 packs?

No. We recommend starting with the factions and expansions your customers are most likely to own.

Can I order expansion bundles?

Yes. Expansion and complete-range assortments can be included in a wholesale quotation.

Are the displayed wholesale prices final?

No. The public page communicates the commercial structure. Final pricing is confirmed in the partner quote.
